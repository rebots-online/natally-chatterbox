# Operator art-direction update — Natally conversation

Robin's additions during the Open Design commission, 17 October 2026:

- Warm, candle-lit atelier, rather than a cold neon or empty cosmic background.
- Many glimmering magical objects, most neatly organized on a shelf. Curated,
  tactile detail: gems, small instruments, books, mysterious trinkets and candle
  glow. Depth and wonder without a cluttered interface.
- A sky of glimmering stars joined by recognizable fine constellation trace-lines.
- The large conversational Natally is **not constrained to a circular portal**.
  Use a full scene and a large free-standing/cutout or layered character. Her
  hands and crystal ball must have room to move. Retain the circular emblem only
  where it serves a compact navigation/avatar role.

Keep warm candle gold and amber as the room's principal illumination; violet
remains in Natally's established turban/cloak and the crystal ball. Face, hands
and ball are the visual focus. Shelf objects glint occasionally at different
intervals, candles vary gently, and constellation shimmer is slow and sparse.
Readable controls/transcript and the chart inset retain calm visual zones.

Keep the actual natal/current-sky chart data contract from the original brief.
Atmospheric background art must not be presented as a live calculation. The
chart's overlay still requires real existing ephemeris data and an as-of time.

Mouth-rig research for the handoff: separate the mouth from the face, provide
small closed/open/wide/rounded poses, and support optional finer visemes later.
Drive speaking motion from the actual audio playback clock/envelope, not queued
text or arbitrary loops. Preserve immediate mouth closure on interruption and
natural closed-mouth Mm/Hmm cues. No new facial ML model/dependency is approved
by the mouth-options discussion; recommendations are recorded separately.
